FreesoundBoards

No board yet. Make one, then add sounds to it.

Results play on click. Add copies the clip and its credit onto the current board. Non-English titles are translated for display; near-identical takes from one pack are folded into a single row.

    Credits

    Freesound clips are Creative Commons. CC BY is a conditional grant — you may use the sound if you credit it. This is that credit, in the form you can paste.

      Sounds from freesound.org, used under the licence each uploader chose. This site stores a cached mp3 preview and the clip's credit; it does not redistribute originals.

      Titles may be translated into English for display. Every credit uses the uploader's original title, because that is what attribution means.

      Board token

      This install protects editing and searching with a shared token (FREESOUND_WRITE_TOKEN in its .env). Playing a board never needs one.
